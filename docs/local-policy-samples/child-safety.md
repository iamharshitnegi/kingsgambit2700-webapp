# Child-safety policy — local development sample

Version: local-test-2026-09-22-v1

This is a sample conduct and safeguarding policy for testing the local KingsGambit2700 portal. It is not evidence of staff vetting, a staffed safeguarding service, or approval for live teaching. Use fictional learner accounts for local tests.

Guardian involvement
A linked guardian reviews the current privacy and child-safety policies before enrollment. Guardians should know the lesson schedule, the assigned coach and the meeting platform, and supervise access in a way appropriate to the learner's age. Keep learner access codes private and share them only with the intended learner.

Respectful teaching
Coaches and learners should use respectful language and age-appropriate chess material. Bullying, harassment, discriminatory remarks, sexual content, threats, humiliation, or pressure to disclose personal information are unacceptable. Learners may stop an activity and ask a trusted adult for help if they feel uncomfortable.

Communication boundaries
Use academy conversations that include authorized guardians and staff oversight for learner-related communication. Do not ask a learner to keep communications secret or move to private social accounts. Do not request passwords, payment details, unrelated photographs, or personal contact information from learners.

Online lessons
Use the assigned classroom and the staff-provided meeting link. Guardians should check the meeting invitation and help learners choose an appropriate background and display name. Do not forward meeting links to uninvited people. The coach confirms attendance. The portal does not record or transcribe lessons; do not record, screenshot or redistribute learner images or conversations during local tests.

Resources and privacy
Share only appropriate academy-provided or original teaching material. Avoid including a child's address, school, phone number or other unnecessary identifying information in game files, homework or screenshots. Access should remain limited to the learner, permitted guardians and authorized staff.

Raising concerns
Leave a meeting or stop an interaction that feels unsafe, and tell a guardian or trusted adult. For test issues, contact the local operator; academy@example.test is a placeholder, not a monitored reporting channel. If a real person faces immediate danger, contact the appropriate local emergency service. Do not investigate allegations yourself or circulate sensitive details in batch announcements.

Response and review
The local operator can restrict access, revoke assignments or devices, and preserve relevant records while a concern is reviewed. Any real academy rollout needs named safeguarding responsibilities and an approved reporting process. Material policy changes require a new published version and renewed guardian acceptance in the portal.
