# Privacy notice — local development sample

Version: local-test-2026-09-22-v1

This sample applies to the local KingsGambit2700 development portal. It is provided to test policy publication and guardian consent; it is not an approved production privacy notice. Use fictional learner and family information.

Operator and contact
The test operator is KingsGambit2700 (Local development). The configured contact is academy@example.test, a test address. Contact the person running this local installation for assistance; this address is not a monitored support service.

Information used
The portal stores account and guardian details, learner names and age bands, country and timezone, family relationships, consent versions, enrollment, class schedules, attendance, learning notes, homework and attempts, uploaded resources, game reviews, reports, certificates, conversations, and credit records. Sign-in and audit records may contain device/browser information, IP addresses, timestamps and security events. Do not upload identity documents, health information or unrelated personal files for testing.

Purpose and access
These records support account access, lessons, practice, family communication, progress reporting, credits and security testing. Students access their own learning records; linked guardians access permitted family records. Assigned coaches and authorized staff access records within their responsibilities. Classroom demonstrations are shared with participants; homework answers and concealed puzzle solutions are not shared as classroom events.

Cookies and services
Essential session and remembered-device cookies maintain sign-in. Signing out or revoking a device invalidates its access. Local email is captured in Mailpit, and uploads use the configured private storage and scanner. Opening a Zoom or Meet link leaves the portal and uses that provider's service. Only open external meeting links when intended.

Retention and requests
Local records remain until the operator removes them or resets the test data; automatic deletion is not promised. Ask the local operator to inspect, correct or remove test records, withdraw recorded consent, or revoke account access. Audit and referenced learning history may require coordinated cleanup. Do not treat a browser-data reset as deletion of database records.

Changes and consent
Policy versions are retained. A replacement privacy or child-safety version requires renewed guardian acceptance for the enrollment workflow. Accept this version only as the linked guardian in the local test scenario.
