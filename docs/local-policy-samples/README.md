# Local policy samples

These samples were published only to the local Compose PostgreSQL database to
exercise policy publication, guardian consent and enrollment. They use clearly
marked test operator/contact values and do not record consent on anyone's behalf.

General drafting references: [ICO privacy-information checklist](https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/uk-gdpr-guidance-and-resources/individual-rights/individual-rights/right-to-be-informed/)
and [NSPCC online safety for schools](https://learning.nspcc.org.uk/online-safety/online-safety-for-schools).
These references inform structure, not a claim that UK rules apply to this academy
or that the samples meet any jurisdiction's legal requirements.
