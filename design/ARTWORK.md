# Approved chess artwork

`public/brand/chess-sculpture-brown.png` (1199 × 1312) is the approved hero artwork.
It was generated with the built-in image tool for the standalone design demo on
8 September 2026, then recolored at the user’s request from cobalt/citrus to warm
brown, sand and ivory. The user subsequently approved application integration.

The artwork shows a ceramic knight and pawn on four raised chess tiles, with
soft studio lighting. The brown edit preserved the original composition,
piece silhouettes, material and lighting. It contains no text or logo.

Colors are baked into this raster image. Changing brand tokens does not recolor
it; an alternate palette needs a matching artwork variant. The original logo is
separate and remains unchanged at `public/logo.png`.
